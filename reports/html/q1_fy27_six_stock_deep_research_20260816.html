Executive conclusion

Quality is abundant.
Margin of safety is not.

BSE, BEL and Coforge are the strongest franchises. India Glycols offers the most valuation flexibility, but with greater cyclicality. Coforge has the clearest quality-growth balance. Syrma posted the strongest quarter, yet carries the most unforgiving expectations.

BSE Limited

Network-effect capital-market infrastructure · Business quality rank #1

Investment case

Liquidity attracts liquidity. BSE’s expanding transaction, data, listing and distribution ecosystem creates rare network effects and exceptional incremental economics.

FY24–FY26 revenue rose from ₹1,568 crore to ₹5,124 crore and PAT from ₹772 crore to ₹2,487 crore. Q1 FY27 extended that step-change, with operating profit reaching ₹1,046 crore. The durability question is whether these economics persist through normal capital-market activity.

What the market prices in

At 49.6× trailing earnings, investors are underwriting sustained derivatives activity, market-share gains and margins above 60%. Product, expiry, fee or regulatory changes can rapidly reset the earnings base.

Investment case

A differentiated technology-services platform with strength in financial services, travel and insurance—and meaningful scale added through acquisition.

FY24–FY26 revenue expanded from ₹9,009 crore to ₹16,403 crore, while PAT rose from ₹836 crore to ₹1,745 crore. Cash conversion is supportive. Acquisition accounting has, however, raised interest and depreciation.

Critical normalization

The official release highlights 110% PAT growth, while the standardized consolidated series shows about 49%. Encora also makes headline YoY growth non-organic. Organic constant-currency growth, integration synergies and cash conversion are the decisive measures.

Investment case

A growing installed base supports recurring service and spares, while India, North America and a European turnaround offer multiple paths to long-term compounding.

FY24–FY26 revenue moved from ₹3,218 crore to ₹3,951 crore and PAT from ₹312 crore to ₹430 crore. FY26 free cash flow was roughly ₹348 crore. Management aspires to become the world’s third-largest compressor company by 2035–36.

Quality caveats

Inventory near 134 days and a cash-conversion cycle near 114 days need discipline. Restructuring expense and a tariff refund should be normalized. Currency, tariffs, input costs and Chinese competition remain live risks.

FY24–FY26 revenue advanced from ₹3,294 crore to ₹4,211 crore and PAT from ₹173 crore to ₹293 crore. Q1 PAT materially outgrew operations because finance cost nearly halved—helpful, but not equivalent to a structural margin expansion.

Investment case

Institutional appendix

What sits beneath the headline

Peer framing, segment drivers, management signals, ownership observations and a transparent three-year analyst model.

BSE

Peer lens: MCX · CDSL · CAMS · NSE (unlisted; closest operating analogue)
Economic segments
Transaction and clearing fees; listings; market data/index licensing; mutual-fund distribution and other market infrastructure.
Core driver
Liquidity and market share, especially derivatives, create strong incremental margins. Non-transaction revenue can reduce cyclicality over time.
Management signal
Current results imply a step-change in scale; the burden of proof is sustained activity after normalizing exceptional market volumes.
Valuation history
Older multiples are a weak anchor because business mix and derivatives economics changed materially. Use normalized mid-cycle earnings rather than a simple historical average.
Governance lens
Professionally managed with no conventional promoter holding. Regulatory dependence and exchange-level controls matter more than promoter pledging.
Red-team question
How much of present profit disappears if derivatives volumes, fee realization or expiry structures normalize?

Coforge

Peer lens: Persistent Systems · LTIMindtree · Mphasis · Hexaware
Economic segments
Banking and financial services, insurance, travel/transport and other digital-engineering services; geographic exposure is predominantly overseas.
Core driver
Large-deal conversion, organic constant-currency growth, utilization and Encora revenue/cost synergies.
Management signal
Official commentary described FY27 as exceptional and Encora integration as complete; reported-versus-adjusted PAT still requires reconciliation.
Valuation history
A premium to mature IT peers is defensible only while growth and deal wins remain superior. Acquisition-aided growth should not receive the same multiple as organic growth.
Governance lens
No promoter holding. FII ownership fell sharply from the March 2025 level in the standardized series; investigate flows, classification and dilution before inferring business deterioration.
Red-team question
What are organic revenue growth and EBIT margin after removing Encora, purchase accounting and integration costs?

Elgi Equipments

Peer lens: Ingersoll-Rand India · Kirloskar Pneumatic · Atlas Copco (global/unlisted India)
Economic segments
Air compressors and related aftermarket; automotive equipment is smaller. Geography-level economics matter more than consolidated revenue alone.
Core driver
India and North America growth, aftermarket penetration and European profitability improvement.
Management signal
Management cited strong inquiry levels and cost action despite geopolitical and input-cost uncertainty; long-term ambition is global #3 by 2035–36.
Valuation history
Recent market screens place its median P/E in the high-40s, above the current low-40s multiple; that does not itself create safety because earnings remain industrial-cycle sensitive.
Governance lens
Track capital allocation across overseas subsidiaries, restructuring outcomes and the economics of divestments. Normalize the disclosed US tariff refund.
Red-team question
Can Europe reach sustainable profitability without continued restructuring while inventory remains elevated?

India Glycols

Peer lens: Jubilant Ingrevia · Godavari Biorefineries · Balrampur Chini · specialty-chemical comparables
Economic segments
Bio/performance chemicals, potable spirits, natural gums and nutraceuticals; no single listed peer reproduces the full mix.
Core driver
Chemical spreads, feedstock economics, plant utilization, spirits profitability, net-debt reduction and post-demerger capital allocation.
Management signal
The NCLT sanctioned the demerger into IGL Spirits and Ennature Bio Pharma on 17 July 2026, moving the thesis from proposal toward execution.
Valuation history
The lowest P/E in the group partly reflects lower ROCE, cyclicality and conglomerate complexity. A rerating needs cleaner segments and durable cash returns.
Governance lens
Promoter holding was 59.63% in June 2026 versus 61.02% through September 2025; DII ownership rose to 5.21%. Scrutinize demerger ratios, debt allocation and related-party arrangements.
Red-team question
Would PAT still grow meaningfully without the ₹20 crore YoY reduction in quarterly finance cost?

Bharat Electronics

Peer lens: HAL · Data Patterns · Astra Microwave · Paras Defence
Economic segments
Defense radars, communications, electronic warfare, naval systems, C4I and weapons electronics; civilian and export businesses diversify a government-heavy base.
Core driver
Order inflow, execution mix, indigenization, exports and the conversion of advances/receivables into operating cash.
Management signal
The official Q1 release showed PBT growth of only 8.81% despite 25% revenue growth, reinforcing margin and mix discipline as the near-term issue.
Valuation history
The strategic premium has expanded alongside India’s defense cycle. At nearly 49× earnings, revenue visibility alone is insufficient—cash conversion and margins must validate the multiple.
Governance lens
Government ownership remained 51.14% in June 2026; FIIs were 18.02% and DIIs 21.02%. State ownership brings strategic access alongside procurement and payment-cycle dependence.
Red-team question
Why did FY26 CFO trail PAT so substantially, and when should working capital reverse?

Syrma SGS Technology

Peer lens: Dixon Technologies · Kaynes Technology · Avalon · Cyient DLM · Amber
Economic segments
EMS and design-led manufacturing across industrial, automotive, healthcare, consumer and other electronics verticals.
Core driver
Customer additions, order conversion, utilization, value-added mix, working capital and returns on new capacity.
Management signal
A CEO transition took effect in June 2026. The operating quarter was excellent, raising—not lowering—the need to prove repeatability and cash conversion.
Valuation history
At 76× trailing earnings, Syrma carries a sector-leader growth multiple despite lower margins and ROCE than the strongest EMS comparables.
Governance lens
Promoter holding declined from 47.27% in March 2023 to 42.28% in June 2026; FIIs held 7.51% and DIIs 15.90%. Determine how much reflects dilution versus selling and what capital returns resulted.
Red-team question
How much revenue is pass-through material value, and what growth remains on a value-added or gross-profit basis?

Decision framework

How these fit a watchlist

No stock offers an unambiguous margin of safety in the base case. Demand either a better price or stronger normalized earnings evidence.

Quality growth

Coforge has the clearest balance, subject to organic-growth and Encora integration proof. Elgi belongs on the long-duration industrial watchlist.

Value / re-rating

India Glycols has the widest rerating possibility, but its earnings are less predictable and corporate-structure analysis is essential.

Price discipline

BSE, BEL and Syrma demonstrate why a great narrative and strong results do not automatically create a favourable risk/reward.
