Elgi Equipments Limited (ELGIEQUIP)

As of 2026-08-14 · Price INR 609.50 · consolidated · Financials in INR crore

Thesis and verdict

  • Business quality: Established air-compressor franchise with a small automotive-equipment leg; India and rest-of-world mix is roughly even, so the business is real but not a local-monopoly utility.
  • Financial quality: Audited FY26 shows rising revenue, PAT and CFO, a net-cash balance sheet, and unmodified PwC opinion; cash conversion is healthy, while capex and two exceptional charges need watching.
  • Growth durability: Q1 FY27 sales grew 23% but the company attributes 7 points to FX; underlying volume/mix is slower, and North America plus India are carrying growth.
  • Valuation comfort: At INR 609.50 on 14 Aug 2026 the stock is about 45x FY26 EPS; the base P/E scenario implies value near the as-of price, so margin of safety is thin.
  • Stance: Watch; research-only, not a personalized recommendation. Prefer evidence of FX-adjusted growth and clean earnings before treating the de-rating as closed.
  • Consolidated air-compressor manufacturer (CIN L29120TZ1960PLC000351) with FY26 revenue INR 3,950.7 crore and PAT INR 430.2 crore, both from the 27 May 2026 audited results.
  • Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 Jun 2026) sales INR 1,062.2 crore and PAT INR 103.3 crore; EBITDA INR 155.3 crore; exceptional restructuring cost INR 7.3 crore pretax.
  • Balance sheet is net cash (presentation: cash INR 1,065.1 crore vs debt INR 385.1 crore at Jun-2026), but FY26 PE is rich versus mid-teens earnings growth.

Business and moat

  • Air compressors are 92% of Q1 FY27 sales mix versus 8% automotive equipment (company presentation).
  • Geographic mix in Q1 was about 49% India / 51% rest of world for compressors; North America sales rose from INR 190.7 crore to INR 260.7 crore YoY in the presentation charts.
  • Distribution and aftermarket depth are asserted by management; this run does not independently verify market share.

Growth drivers

  • Q1 FY27 revenue +23% YoY, of which +7% is exchange-driven per the 14 Aug 2026 presentation.
  • India standalone sales INR 644.8 crore vs INR 502.7 crore in Q1 FY26; Europe INR 151.4 crore vs INR 124.8 crore.
  • FY26 other income includes INR 37.2 crore property-sale gain; that is not a recurring operating driver.

Risks

  • Downside first: valuation at ~45x FY26 EPS leaves little room if FX tailwinds reverse or if US/India demand slows.
  • Q1 EBITDA missed a volume-implied INR 170.3 crore because of higher employee cost (INR 8.8 crore) and other expenses (INR 16.9 crore).
  • Exceptional items: FY26 labour-code past-service cost INR 15.0 crore; Q1 FY27 restructuring INR 7.3 crore. Further labour-code rules are still being monitored per the FY26 notes.
  • Screener flags possible capitalization of interest cost; not verified from the audit notes in this pass.
  • Capex stepped up: INR 48.9 crore (FY24) → 94.8 (FY25) → 154.4 (FY26); Q1 FY27 presentation shows INR 34.8 crore capex in the net-cash walk.

Governance

  • Statutory auditors Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP issued an unmodified opinion on FY26 standalone and consolidated results (board outcome letter 27 May 2026).
  • Promoter holding 31.19% is flat across the Screener series to Jun 2026; FII holding fell from 28.82% (Sep 2023) to 21.90% (Jun 2026) while DII rose from 4.84% to 10.12%.
  • 66th AGM resolutions 1–7 passed on 14 Aug 2026 including dividend (BSE scrutinizer filing).

Segments and geography

  • FY26 segment revenue: air compressors INR 3,613.4 crore vs INR 3,211.8 crore; automotive equipment INR 338.6 crore vs INR 301.5 crore (audited segment annexure, 27 May 2026).
  • Q1 FY27 compressor/auto mix 92%/8%; India/ROW compressor mix 49%/51% (14 Aug 2026 presentation).

Peer and industry context

  • Screener peer table was empty on 16 Aug 2026; no like-for-like peer multiples established from current evidence.

Management commentary and execution

  • Management presents Q1 growth as 23% with 7% FX. Presentation EBITDA bridge: volume-implied INR 170.3 crore vs delivered INR 155.3 crore after cost inflation.
  • Claim of resilient performance is only partly supported: reported growth is real, but a material slice is currency and Q1 PAT margin 9.7% vs 9.9% a year ago.

Shareholding and ownership signals

  • Jun 2026 (Screener): promoters 31.19%, FII 21.90%, DII 10.12%, public 36.30%, others 0.49%. No promoter pledge identified in this scrape.
  • FII reduction of about 7 percentage points since Sep 2023 is the main ownership signal; DII has absorbed part of that.

Red-team questions

  • If the INR strengthens, does Q1-type 23% growth collapse toward high-single-digit, breaking the 15.5 forward-EPS base case?
  • Are labour-code and restructuring charges finished, or will FY27 earnings stay noisy?
  • Does rising capex (and CWIP on Screener) precede a period of lower FCF that the 38–44x bull/base multiples cannot absorb?
