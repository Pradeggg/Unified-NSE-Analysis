# Fundamental Analysis — Simple Tareeke Se

## Series concept

**Fundamental Analysis — Simple Tareeke Se** is a beginner-friendly Agent Adda education series that explains how to understand a company’s business, financial statements, management quality, risks, and valuation without unnecessary jargon.

**Tagline:**

> Business samjho. Numbers padho. Risk pehchaano. Decision khud lo.

The series uses short conversations between **Bhatti** and **Chaddha**, supported by simple visuals and real-company examples.

## Audience

- New investors learning how to read a company.
- Retail investors who know basic market terms but struggle with financial statements.
- Viewers who want a repeatable research process instead of stock tips.

## Learning journey

Every episode should answer one of four questions:

1. Company paisa kaise kamati hai?
2. Paisa sach mein bach raha hai ya nahi?
3. Company growth kar sakti hai ya nahi?
4. Stock ki current price reasonable hai ya nahi?

## Episode roadmap

### Part 1 — Business samjho

1. **Company karti kya hai?**
   - Products, customers, industry, segments, business model.

2. **Company ka competitive advantage kya hai?**
   - Brand, cost advantage, distribution, technology, switching costs.

3. **Growth kahan se aa rahi hai?**
   - Volume, pricing, new products, market share, acquisitions.

### Part 2 — Profit samjho

4. **Sales badhi, par profit kyun nahi?**
   - Revenue, EBITDA, PAT, expenses, operating margin.

5. **Margin ka asli meaning**
   - Gross margin, EBITDA margin, net margin, cyclical businesses.

6. **Profit asli hai ya paper profit?**
   - CFO, free cash flow, working capital, profit-to-cash conversion.

### Part 3 — Financial health check

7. **Balance sheet ka medical report**
   - Debt, cash, receivables, inventory, working capital.

8. **ROE aur ROCE ko simple banao**
   - Capital efficiency, borrowed growth, quality growth.

9. **Management par kitna bharosa?**
   - Promoter holding, pledging, related parties, dilution, capital allocation.

### Part 4 — Stock analysis

10. **Quarterly results kaise padhein?**
    - QoQ, YoY, one-off items, guidance, trends.

11. **Achhi company, mehenga stock**
    - P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, PEG, peer comparison.

12. **Final 10-minute company checklist**
    - Business, moat, growth, profit, cash, debt, management, valuation, risks, triggers.

## Standard episode format

Each episode should follow a recognisable structure:

1. **Hook** — a market myth or common mistake.
2. **Bhatti’s question** — the beginner question viewers are thinking.
3. **Chaddha’s explanation** — plain Hindi/Hinglish, one concept at a time.
4. **Real-company example** — clearly dated and labelled as educational.
5. **Visual analogy** — household budget, factory, water tank, shop, or medical report.
6. **Green flag** — what healthy evidence looks like.
7. **Red flag** — what requires further investigation.
8. **Three-question takeaway** — a repeatable mini-checklist.
9. **Research disclaimer** — included in every episode and distribution format.

## Characters

### Bhatti

The curious beginner. Bhatti asks direct questions, challenges jargon, and represents the viewer’s doubts.

### Chaddha

The calm explainer. Chaddha breaks complicated ideas into practical examples without making predictions or giving personal instructions.

### The Company

Appears as a character whose business, profits, cash, debt, and risks can be examined.

### The Auditor

Checks whether reported profit is supported by operating cash flow.

### The Market

Represents price, expectations, sentiment, valuation, and changing risk appetite.

## Simple analogies

| Finance concept | Everyday analogy |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Total household income |
| Operating expenses | Household running costs |
| Profit | Money left after expenses |
| Cash flow | Money actually received in the bank |
| Debt | Borrowed money that must be repaid |
| Receivables | Money customers still owe the company |
| Inventory | Goods sitting unsold in the storeroom |
| ROE | Return earned on shareholders’ money |
| ROCE | Return earned from all capital used |
| Valuation | Price paid for the business |
| Promoter pledge | Owner borrowing against their shares |

## Company Health Card

End every episode with a compact scorecard:

```text
🏢 Business: Strong / Average / Weak
📈 Growth: Improving / Stable / Declining
💰 Profit quality: Strong / Mixed / Weak
💵 Cash conversion: Healthy / Watch / Concern
🏦 Debt: Low / Manageable / High
👔 Management: Clean / Needs review / Unclear
💸 Valuation: Reasonable / Expensive / Unclear
⚠️ Biggest risk: {RISK}
📅 Data as of: {DATE}
```

The card is a discussion aid, not an investment rating.

## Research workflow taught by the series

```text
Understand the business
        ↓
Check growth and margins
        ↓
Reconcile profit with cash
        ↓
Review debt and balance-sheet health
        ↓
Assess management and governance
        ↓
Compare valuation with quality and peers
        ↓
Write the thesis, risks, and invalidation points
```

## Editorial guardrails

- Use real companies as teaching examples, not as automatic recommendations.
- State the data date and distinguish live, delayed, and historical information.
- Separate reported facts, model outputs, management claims, and assumptions.
- Explain both positive evidence and what could go wrong.
- Use “watchpoint”, “risk”, “scenario”, and “invalidation” instead of directive language.
- Never imply assured, fixed, or guaranteed returns.
- Do not use the series to provide personalised buy, sell, or hold instructions.
- Verify figures against company filings, exchange disclosures, and current market data.

## Launch sequence

The recommended first five episodes are:

1. Company karti kya hai?
2. Sales aur profit ka farq
3. Profit asli hai ya paper profit?
4. Debt aur balance sheet
5. Valuation simple tareeke se

This sequence creates an intuitive foundation before introducing more technical ratios.
