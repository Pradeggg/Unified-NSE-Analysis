# Midcap Leaders Portfolio Policy

Date: 2026-08-08
Status: Draft internal model-portfolio research policy
Vehicle type: Internal paper/model portfolio, not a mutual fund, PMS, AIF, advisory product, or pooled investment vehicle
Reference capital: Rs. 5,00,000 paper NAV
Owners: Mahesh Binjola and Pradeep Gorai

## Mandate

Build a repeatable Agent Adda midcap portfolio process across Nifty Midcap 50, Nifty Midcap 100, Nifty Midcap 150, Midcap Select, and LargeMidcap/Nifty 500 confirmation layers.

The portfolio is designed to find midcap companies where market structure, earnings acceleration, sector leadership, and government-investment themes align. It is research-first and paper-only until evidence, trigger, stop, liquidity, and governance gates are cleared.

## Required Selection Gates

- Stage 2 or clear Stage 2 proxy.
- Growth score above policy threshold.
- High EPS or earnings-quality proxy above threshold.
- YoY sales-growth score above threshold.
- Sector theme aligned with current market leadership.
- Government-investment alignment for capital goods, defence, infrastructure, manufacturing, energy, housing, logistics, PSU-credit, or digital infrastructure.
- Liquidity sufficient for paper execution.
- No-chase rule when RSI or price extension is excessive.
- Fresh official result and filing source trail before any paper order.

## Portfolio Rules

- Initial deployment cap: Rs. 2,00,000.
- Target positions: 6-8.
- Initial slot: Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 25,000.
- Maximum validated slot: Rs. 45,000 to Rs. 50,000.
- Sector cap: 25% of NAV.
- Single-entry risk: 0.50% to 0.75% of NAV.
- Total open risk cap: 6% of NAV.
- Minimum reward/risk: 2:1.
- No averaging down.
- No paper order from theme or score alone.

## Decision Buckets

- CORE CANDIDATE: all main gates align, but still needs live trigger and official refresh.
- WATCH / PREPARE: usable candidate but missing setup, theme, or score strength.
- RETEST ONLY: strong candidate but extended.
- REFRESH FIRST: missing or stale EPS/sales/fundamental evidence.
- NO ORDER: trigger touched while blockers remain.

## Government-Investment Theme Map

- Defence: indigenisation, procurement, aerospace and electronics supply chains.
- Infrastructure: public capex, roads, rail, ports, utilities, engineering.
- Manufacturing: PLI, Make in India, electronics, industrial capex.
- EV and mobility: electric vehicles, auto components, batteries, charging ecosystem.
- Transportation and logistics: rail, multimodal logistics, ports, warehousing.
- Housing and urban infrastructure: housing, building products, real estate services.
- Energy: power, grid, energy transition, gas infrastructure.
- Public credit cycle: PSU banks and government-linked credit transmission.
- Digital infrastructure: digital public infrastructure, platforms, enterprise digitisation.

## Operating Stance

This policy creates a research and paper-trading workflow. It does not approve live investment, external solicitation, pooled capital, or regulated fund activity.
