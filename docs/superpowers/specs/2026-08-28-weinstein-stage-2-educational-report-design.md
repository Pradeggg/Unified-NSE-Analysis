# Weinstein Stage 2 Educational Report — Design Specification (v2)

## Approved editorial decisions

- **Disclaimer and sign-off line.** Agent Adda LinkedIn content uses "Knowledge is
  the MOAT — Hold, Think, Act" in place of a formal disclaimer footer. This report
  makes specific historical stock/price claims on a public page — higher risk
  surface than a LinkedIn post. Recommendation: keep **both** — full SEBI
  research-only disclaimer for compliance, brand sign-off line for identity —
  rather than one replacing the other. The report will include both.
- **Naming the failed-breakout company.** Using a real, identifiable company for
  the "bull trap" case study needs framing that separates chart-pattern behavior
  from any implication about the company's fundamentals or quality. Confirm
  company fundamentals or quality. A real historical company may be named when
  this neutral framing is present.

## Purpose

Create a public, evergreen Agent Adda educational report that teaches readers how
to recognise Weinstein Stage 2. The report must explain the original framework
faithfully, demonstrate it with historical NSE charts, and clearly separate Agent
Adda's modern confirmation tools from Weinstein's original method.

The report is educational. It must not present current stock recommendations or
imply that a chart pattern guarantees a return.

## Audience and reading level

The primary reader is a self-directed investor with secondary-school education
and limited charting experience. Intermediate readers may use optional deep-dive
sections.

**Target length:** ~15-20 minute core read (sections 1-10), with deep-dives and
the printable checklist (11-12) as optional add-on time. Cut or tighten any
section that pushes the core read past ~20 minutes.

All copy must be in English and understandable to a Grade 12 reader:

- Define a technical term before using it, **and** maintain it in the persistent
  glossary (see Interaction and accessibility) — first-use definitions alone
  don't survive readers jumping in via the sticky contents bar.
- Prefer short sentences and familiar words.
- Explain examples before formulas.
- Keep facts, interpretation, and risk clearly separated.
- Use plain alternatives to trading jargon where possible.
- Add a short "Why this matters" explanation to every teaching chart.

## Working title

**Weinstein Stage 2: A Simple Guide to Recognising a Strong Uptrend**

## Core promise

After reading the report, a reader should be able to:

1. Place a stock broadly within Weinstein's four-stage cycle.
2. Recognise the minimum evidence for a possible Stage 2 advance.
3. Identify common false signals and late-stage risks.
4. Explain the difference between Weinstein's original rules and Agent Adda's
   additions.
5. Use a checklist for further research without treating it as a buy
   recommendation.

## Editorial structure

1. **Hero and scope** — title, one-sentence promise, educational label, and
   reading guide.
2. **The framework in 60 seconds** — a single visual showing Stages 1-4 and the
   role of the 30-week moving average.
3. **What Stage 2 means** — price trend, rising 30-week average, breakout,
   volume, relative strength, and reduced overhead supply.
4. **Recognition checklist** — qualifying evidence and disqualifying evidence
   shown side by side.
5. **Historical case studies** — full lifecycle, clean breakout, failed
   breakout, and mature/exhausted Stage 2.
6. **Original Weinstein method** — faithful summary with primary-source
   attribution. Name the source directly: Stan Weinstein, *Secrets for Profiting
   in Bull and Bear Markets* — paraphrase only, one short quote per source
   maximum, under 15 words, per standard copyright practice.
7. **Agent Adda modern confirmation layer** — daily moving averages, VCP,
   Supertrend, fundamentals, breadth, and liquidity.
8. **Original versus modern** — a side-by-side comparison that prevents source
   confusion.
9. **Risk discipline** — entry zone, invalidation, position-risk concept, exit
   logic, and gap risk.
10. **Common mistakes** — chasing, weak-volume breakouts, overhead supply,
    confusing Stage 1 with Stage 2, and ignoring market conditions.
11. **Printable checklist and decision flow** — a one-page learning aid.
12. **Methodology, sources, limitations, and disclaimer** — including the
    selection-bias disclosure below.

## Evidence and attribution

- Use primary or authoritative sources for Weinstein's original framework
  wherever accessible.
- Attribute paraphrased ideas; do not reproduce substantial copyrighted book
  text.
- Label every Agent Adda rule as a modern addition rather than an original
  Weinstein rule.
- Use historical NSE price data from the project database for examples.
- Freeze each case study at a stated historical date to avoid hindsight
  ambiguity.
- Show symbol, exchange, timeframe, data range, and source beneath each chart.
- Label every example: "Historical educational case study — not a current
  recommendation."
- **Tag every factual claim and chart with a Sourced / Indicative / To Verify
  badge**, matching the system used on the Ford client deliverables. Sourced =
  directly traceable to NSE data or a cited primary source. Indicative =
  reasonable inference not independently verified. To Verify = flagged for
  follow-up before publish; nothing in this tier ships unresolved.
- **Disclose selection bias explicitly** in the Methodology section: case
  studies were chosen after their outcomes were known, for teaching clarity —
  not identified in real time. State plainly that this is a known limitation of
  any pattern-education content, and that recognising Stage 2 as it forms is
  harder than recognising it in hindsight.

## Historical example selection

Use a deliberate mix of recognisable large-cap companies and visually clear
mid/small-cap examples. Select examples by pattern quality, data completeness,
and teaching value — not by recent performance alone.

Required chart set:

1. One full four-stage lifecycle.
2. One clean Stage 2 breakout with supportive volume and relative strength.
3. One failed breakout or bull trap. Framing must separate chart-pattern
   behavior from any judgment on company quality.
4. One late or mature Stage 2 example showing deterioration.
5. One side-by-side chart comparing the original framework with Agent Adda
   confirmations.

Document the case-study dates, symbols, and selection reasons after reviewing
the historical evidence and before generating final charts. Avoid presenting an
active setup as the main teaching example. Note in the record that single
examples are illustrative, not a statistical sample — the report should say so
explicitly wherever a reader might over-generalise from one company's chart.

## Chart design

Use the Agent Adda TradingView chart workflow with NSE symbol resolution. Use
weekly charts for the original 30-week framework and daily charts only for the
modern confirmation layer.

The standard Agent Adda daily studies are SMA 20, SMA 50, SMA 200, RSI 14, MACD
12/26/9, Supertrend 10x3, ADX 14, and volume. Do not imply that all are
Weinstein indicators.

Teaching annotations should mark only the essential points:

- Stage boundaries.
- Base resistance and breakout.
- Direction of the 30-week average.
- Volume expansion or weakness.
- Relative-strength confirmation or divergence.
- Invalidation or failed-breakout area.

With six possible annotation types per chart, inline labels will crowd the price
action on mobile. **Use numbered markers on the chart with a caption legend
below it**, rather than dense inline text, so the chart stays readable at small
sizes and in print.

Charts must remain readable on mobile and in print. Use captions rather than
dense labels when annotations would obscure price.

## Visual language

Follow the Agent Adda editorial theme:

- Deep navy for structure and headings.
- Muted blue for neutral explanation.
- Emerald for constructive Stage 2 evidence.
- Amber for caution and incomplete confirmation.
- Red only for invalidation, failure, or material risk.
- White cards, restrained shadows, clear spacing, and strong typographic
  hierarchy.
- Evidence chips for timeframe, source, and historical date — extend these to
  carry the Sourced / Indicative / To Verify tag as well.

The page should feel like an illustrated learning guide, not a trading terminal
or a daily screener.

## Original framework versus Agent Adda layer

The report must maintain a visible boundary:

| Original Weinstein concepts | Agent Adda modern additions |
|---|---|
| Four-stage cycle | Automated stage scoring |
| Weekly price chart | Daily confirmation chart |
| 30-week moving average | SMA 150 and SMA 200 alignment |
| Breakout above resistance | VCP and consolidation measurements |
| Volume confirmation | Supertrend, RSI, MACD, and ADX |
| Relative strength | Market breadth and sector rotation |
| Buying-zone and stop discipline | Fundamental quality and liquidity checks |

Agent Adda additions may strengthen research discipline, but they must not be
described as mandatory Weinstein rules.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Provide a sticky contents bar on desktop and a compact jump menu on mobile.
- Make optional intermediate-level explanations expandable.
- **Provide a persistent glossary** (sidebar on desktop, expandable drawer on
  mobile) covering every defined term — required because the jump menu lets
  readers skip past the section where a term was first introduced.
- Use semantic headings, descriptive chart captions, sufficient colour
  contrast, and text equivalents for colour-coded states.
- Add alt text to every chart image describing what it shows, not just its
  filename, and ensure the jump menu and expandable sections are fully
  keyboard-navigable.
- Do not hide essential teaching or risk information inside interactions.
- Ensure the standalone HTML remains self-contained or uses publish-safe
  same-origin assets.

## Quality gates

Before publication, verify:

- Every technical term is defined at first use and present in the glossary.
- All historical dates and prices agree with the source data.
- Weekly and daily timeframes are clearly labelled.
- Original and modern rules are not mixed.
- Every chart has a source, date range, caption, evidence-tier badge, and
  educational label.
- **Run a readability check (Flesch-Kincaid or equivalent) against the Grade 12
  target** rather than relying on editorial judgment alone.
- **Confirm chart image redistribution complies with the charting library's
  (TradingView) terms for public embedding.**
- No local `file://` links, placeholders, raw JSON, missing images, or visible
  NaN values exist.
- The Agent Adda/SEBI research-only disclaimer is present and prominent,
  alongside rather than replaced by the brand sign-off line.
- The report renders cleanly on desktop, mobile, and print/PDF.
- External citations resolve and the public page plus standalone HTML return
  HTTP 200.
- The core read (sections 1-10) stays within the ~15-20 minute target.

## Deliverables

- Source research notes with citation trail.
- Historical example-selection record.
- Agent Adda chart artifacts.
- Standalone HTML report.
- Optional PDF derived from the validated HTML.
- Website MDX metadata and public report page.
- Short WhatsApp and email announcement copy, prepared but not sent without
  confirmation.

## Out of scope

- Live buy/sell calls.
- Personal portfolio advice.
- Intraday or options strategies.
- Claims that Stage 2 predicts returns with certainty.
- A full quantitative backtest of every Weinstein rule. A future report may
  test performance separately.

## Freshness policy

Evergreen framework, dated examples — the two need different treatment.

- Record a **"last reviewed"** date on the published page.
- Re-audit triggers: any corporate action (split, bonus, merger) affecting a
  featured symbol's historical price series; annually at minimum otherwise.
- Re-audit checks that adjusted historical prices still match what's shown in
  the frozen case-study charts.

## Acceptance criteria

The report is complete when a Grade 12 reader can correctly describe Stage 2,
distinguish a clean breakout from a weak one, identify the main risks, and
explain which indicators belong to Weinstein versus Agent Adda. All factual
claims and charts must be traceable and tagged, the readability check must pass
the Grade 12 target, QA must pass, the website build must succeed, and both
public URL forms must be verified live.
