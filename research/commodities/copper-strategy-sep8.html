Macro Context — Why Copper is at ATH
Supply Squeeze
  • Chile — production down 9.4% YoY in July; world's largest copper producer
  • Congo — export ban disrupting Central African refined copper supply
  • Peru — intermittent El-Niño disruptions weighing on output
  • LME inventory outside US declining; global warehouse stock near multi-year lows
  • No major greenfield mines coming online before 2028–29
Demand Acceleration
  • AI data centres — 10–15× more copper per MW than conventional compute
  • Power grid upgrades — renewables, EV charging, transmission buildout
  • EVs — 4× copper per vehicle vs ICE; adoption accelerating
  • US tariff fears creating COMEX inventory build (COMEX–LME spread positive)
  • Goldman estimates structural demand deficit through 2030
Broker Views
  • Goldman Sachs — "Prices forecast to decline somewhat from record highs"; medium-term supply response expected but bull trend intact
  • JP Morgan — Structurally bullish on secular demand; near-term RSI extended
  • Sprott — "Catalysts intact for 2026 — supply deficit not resolved"
  • Consensus: pullbacks are opportunities within the multi-year bull
Risks to Watch
  • China PMI — any slowdown = demand headwind; monthly release is key trigger
  • DXY — USD strength historically caps copper; watch for reversal
  • US tariff policy — escalation = supply chain disruption, not demand boost
  • RSI extended at 70 — historically 3–7% pullback before continuation
  • WTI crude at $92 — energy cost uncertainty weighs on industrial CAPEX
