Root-Cause Analysis — The Five Drivers

01

🌐 US 10-Year Yield at 4.80% — the primary trigger

US 10Y has jumped +3.4% in 5 days to 4.80% — a level where EM equity outflows accelerate as risk-free return competes directly with Indian equity risk premium. DXY at 99.56 strengthens the dollar, making USD-denominated returns more attractive vs INR-denominated assets. Historically, when the US 10Y breaches 4.5%, FII net selling in India picks up within 1–2 weeks. The INR at 94.96 is a multi-year high vs USD — each rupee depreciation makes repatriated USD returns larger, incentivising FII to hold rather than add. The Nikkei's −3.0% drop today is consistent with JPY carry-trade unwinding as US rates stay high — that same carry unwind hits EM equities simultaneously.

IMPACT: HIGH TRANSMISSION: 2–3 week lag
02

🛢️ Crude Oil Shock — Brent +8.5% in 5 days

Brent at $95.28 (+8.5% in 5 days), WTI at $90.70 (+10.3% in 5 days). India imports ~87% of crude requirements — every $10/barrel rise in oil adds ~₹1.1–1.4 Lakh Cr to the annual import bill, widens the current account deficit by ~0.4% GDP, and pushes retail fuel prices up with a typical 2–4 week lag. Direct sector casualties: Auto (−2.69% yesterday) — raw material cost hike (plastics, rubber, lubricants) + demand softness as consumers defer purchases on higher fuel costs; Airlines — ATF price spike; Paints/Chemicals — petrochemical feedstock costs. Paradoxically benefiting: Energy (+0.59% today), ONGC, Oil India, MRPL (upstream producers get higher realizations). The sector breadth data confirms Energy PSUs holding (PSE +0.37% today).

IMPACT: HIGH SECTORS HIT: Auto, Airlines, Chemicals, FMCG margins
03

📊 Breadth Catastrophe — Most Stocks Already in Decline

This is not a sudden crash — it is a long-running distribution. Stage 3 (topping) = 42.8% of all stocks. Stage 4 (declining) = 20.6%. Combined 63.4% of the market is already deteriorating. Only 14.6% of stocks remain in Stage 2 uptrend. The AD oscillator hit −71.3 on Sep 1 — deeply negative territory. Breadth has been negative (advances < declines) on 10 of the last 15 trading sessions. TRIN at 1.16 on Sep 1 confirms sell-side volume dominance. The McClellan oscillator and AD ratio are both in contraction. Sector breadth (as of Aug 21) shows Energy at just 23.7% above 50dma, FMCG at 26.7% — these are distribution-phase levels. The rally of Aug 26 (1,450 advances) was a dead-cat bounce that failed to follow through.

IMPACT: HIGH — structural, multi-week
04

🔄 ROTATION Regime — HMM Confirms Phase Shift

The Gaussian HMM regime classifier entered ROTATION on Aug 21. In this regime, the model sees money rotating out of momentum/growth (IT, Auto) and into defensives (Pharma, PSU/Energy). Evidence: Pharma was the only major index in the green yesterday (−0.05%), Energy +0.59% today, Realty +0.24% — classic late-bull-market rotation. IT at 100% of stocks above 50dma but the index itself falling −1.25% today suggests profit-booking in winners even as technicals remain intact. In a ROTATION regime, Agent Adda's signal weights shift: BUY signals require higher tech scores, STRONG_BUY thresholds tighten, and the regime overlay reduces notional position sizing by 20–30%.

IMPACT: MEDIUM — phase signal, not panic DURATION: Typically 2–6 weeks before resolving to BULL or BEAR
05

🗓️ Global Risk-Off — Japan, Europe Selling

Nikkei −3.0% today, −2.32% in 5 days — consistent with JPY carry trade unwinding (higher US rates = JPY strengthens = leveraged positions unwind = EM selling). DAX −1.62% in 5 days, FTSE −0.90% — European equity weakness. When developed-market institutions sell their EM allocation to de-risk portfolios, India gets sold alongside. Gold is also −3.56% in 5 days despite being a safe haven — suggesting forced liquidation (institutions selling profitable gold to cover losses elsewhere) rather than genuine risk-on. The combination of oil up + gold down + DXY up = classic global tightening panic.

IMPACT: MEDIUM — amplifier, not primary driver

Green Shoots — What Could Reverse The Fall

🛢️
Crude stabilises below $85
A mean-reversion in crude (supply increase, demand drop) would instantly re-rate Auto, FMCG, Airlines, Chemicals. Would reduce CAD pressure on INR.
📉
US 10Y backs off below 4.5%
Any dovish Fed signal or weak US jobs data could pull yields down, reducing EM outflow pressure. DXY would weaken → INR stabilises → FII buying resumes.
🏦
DII floor is solid
₹41,643 Cr DII buying in 30 days is structural. Mutual fund SIP flows (~₹20,000+ Cr/month) create a monthly price floor. This prevents a crash — but not a grind.
💊
Pharma near 52W high (93% range)
Defensive rotation visible. Pharma holding strong even as broader market falls — showing institutional preference for earnings visibility over growth risk.
🏛️
RBI rate cut catalyst
If RBI signals an October rate cut (next MPC Sep 30 — Oct 2), domestic rate-sensitives (Banks, Realty, NBFCs) could rally strongly and pull indices up.
📊
Q2 earnings (Oct–Nov)
Q2FY27 earnings season (Oct) is the next fundamental catalyst. If IT and Banks beat estimates, the correction could find a durable floor.

📋 Research Summary — What To Do

  • Do not fight the trend. 63% of stocks in Stage 3/4, ROTATION regime active, breadth deeply negative on 10/15 sessions. The weight of evidence is bearish.
  • Primary macro triggers remain live. US 10Y at 4.80% and crude at $95 Brent are not resolved. Until at least one reverses, macro headwind persists.
  • DII prevents a crash. The ₹41,643 Cr DII support in 30 days means this is a grind, not a collapse. Nifty's 52W floor (22,183) is well defended by institutional buying programs.
  • Rotate defensively. Pharma (near 52W high, relative strength), Energy/PSU (crude tailwind), Realty (rate-cut optionality) — these are the pockets working. Reduce Auto, IT exposure at bounces.
  • STP discipline matters here. Only trade setups with R:R ≥ 2.0, hard stops, and Stage 2 confirmation. POWERGRID and COALINDIA showed the right pattern this morning — isolated pockets of strength in a weak market.
  • Watch Sep 30 – Oct 2 RBI MPC. A rate-cut signal is the highest-probability single catalyst to reverse the correction. If crude pulls back AND RBI cuts → Nifty could snap back 2–3% in a session.
