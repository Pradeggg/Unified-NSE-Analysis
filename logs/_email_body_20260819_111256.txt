    Weinstein stage framework

    Stage 1 - Base / Accumulation: sideways action after a decline; watchlist phase.

    Stage 2 - Advancing / Uptrend: breakout above the base with rising averages and improving relative strength; preferred long-only buying zone.

    Stage 3 - Top / Distribution: volatility near highs and fading momentum; caution phase.

    Stage 4 - Decline / Downtrend: price below key averages with lower highs/lows; usually avoided by long-only systems.
