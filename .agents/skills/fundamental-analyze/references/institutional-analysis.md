# Institutional analysis extension

Use this extension only for deep-research or shareable-report requests.

## Evidence blocks

- Segment and geography: revenue mix, margin mix, capacity, utilization, pricing, market share, and currency exposure.
- Peer context: comparable business scope, period, growth, margins, returns, leverage, and valuation. Explain exclusions.
- Management execution: separate claims from delivered outcomes; compare guidance with subsequent filings.
- Ownership: promoter holding and pledge, FII/DII trend, dilution, insider transactions, and material related parties.
- Forecasts: identify the period, base figures, operational assumptions, earnings bridge, and failure conditions.
- Red team: state the strongest bear case, accounting-quality concern, disconfirming evidence, and thesis breakers.

Put sourced observations into the optional `institutional` object described in `input-schema.md`. Do not turn a missing field into a confident statement. Keep every claim traceable to the source list and make assumptions visibly different from reported facts.
