agentadda.in · NSE Equities & Derivatives Research Platform

Agent Adda is a research and education tool for Indian equity markets. This policy explains, in plain language, what data we handle, how conversations are stored, and what we never do. There are no dark patterns here.

What Agent Adda is

Agent Adda is a research-grade equities and derivatives analysis platform built for the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE). It is designed for retail investors, traders, and students who want to do serious market research without needing a Bloomberg terminal or a team of analysts.

The platform has two interfaces:

  • Talk 2 Stocks — a conversational web interface where you ask questions in plain English and receive structured market analysis: stock stage classification, technical scores, fundamental sub-scores, options data, screener results, and market breadth.
  • Agent Adda CLI — a terminal REPL for deeper research, running the same analysis engine with additional commands for backtesting, voice briefings, PDF reports, and sector rotation tracking.

Under the hood, Agent Adda runs a daily data pipeline that ingests NSE bhavcopy (end-of-day price files), F&O (futures & options) data, corporate events, FII/DII flows, and company fundamentals from public sources. This data is scored using a rules-based engine covering Weinstein stage analysis, technical momentum, and five fundamental sub-scores (earnings quality, sales growth, financial strength, institutional backing, and a composite enhanced score). An LLM layer synthesises these signals into readable research summaries.

Agent Adda covers approximately 1,500 NSE-listed stocks and indices including NIFTY 50, NIFTY BANK, NIFTY MIDCAP, and NIFTY 500 constituents, along with ~40 built-in screeners (Stage 2 leaders, VCP setups, high relative-strength movers, quality breakouts, and more).

It does not provide investment advice. All outputs are educational and informational. No analysis constitutes a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security. Agent Adda is not registered with SEBI as an investment advisor.

What we collect

  • Session identifiers — a random ID generated in your browser when you open Talk 2 Stocks. It is not linked to your name, email, or device fingerprint.
  • Conversation turns — your questions and the answers returned within a session are held in server memory only for the duration of that session (up to 1 hour of inactivity). They are not written to a persistent database or log file.
  • Watchlist symbols — stored only in your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device unless you include them in a question.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, financial positions, brokerage account details, or any other personally identifying information.

How conversations are handled

Each browser session gets its own isolated conversation context. Sessions are evicted from memory after 1 hour of inactivity — after which no record of the conversation is retained on our servers.

When you send a question that requires AI synthesis, the question text and relevant market data are sent to OpenAI's API to generate a response. See the Third-party services section below for details.

Market data requests (prices, filings, breadth) go directly to NSE's public APIs and Screener.in. Your question text is not forwarded to those sources.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your conversation data with third parties
  • We do not build a profile of your investment interests or behaviour
  • We do not use your data to train AI models
  • We do not place advertising or tracking cookies
  • We do not require account registration or collect credentials
  • We do not retain conversation history after your session expires
  • We do not provide personalised investment advice or act as a SEBI-registered advisor

Third-party services

Agent Adda depends on the following external services:

OpenAI
AI synthesis for research answers (GPT-4o / GPT-5). Your question text is sent to OpenAI when AI generation is used. OpenAI's data handling is governed by their privacy policy ↗
NSE India
Live and end-of-day price data, index data, and market breadth via NSE's public APIs. No personal data is transmitted.
Screener.in
Fundamental data and quarterly results for NSE-listed companies. Data is fetched server-side; your identity is not shared.
Yahoo Finance
Historical OHLCV data via the yfinance library for back-fill and extended price history. No personal data is transmitted.

Browser storage

Talk 2 Stocks uses localStorage to remember your watchlist and UI preferences (e.g. collapsed sidebar) between visits. This data stays in your browser. You can clear it at any time via your browser's "Clear site data" setting — it has no effect on our servers.

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or cross-site identifiers of any kind.

Data retention

Conversation sessions — evicted from server memory after 1 hour of inactivity. No persistent copy is made.

Market data caches — price, earnings, and breadth data are cached server-side for 18–24 hours to reduce API load. These caches contain only publicly available market data — no user information.

Server logs — our web server may log standard HTTP request metadata (timestamp, endpoint, response code, approximate request size). IP addresses, if logged, are not linked to conversation content and are rotated within 7 days.

Your choices

  • Clear your session — close the browser tab. The session expires within 1 hour and is purged from server memory.
  • Clear local data — use your browser's site data settings to remove localStorage entries for agentadda.in.
  • Opt out of AI synthesis — if you prefer answers from deterministic rules only (no OpenAI calls), set the environment variable T2S_USE_AGENT_BRIDGE=0 when running a self-hosted instance.

Changes to this policy
